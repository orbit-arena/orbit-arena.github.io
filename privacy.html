Privacy policy

Last updated 2026-11-08

Orbit Arena ("we") is an independent creator of original physics-simulation videos, operated by Aymen Jallouli in Tunisia. This policy explains what information our website and our internal publishing tool, Orbit Arena Studio, handle. Questions: factoryballrace@gmail.com.

Our website

This website has no accounts, forms, cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may process technical data such as IP addresses to deliver and secure the site, under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Orbit Arena Studio

Orbit Arena Studio is software we run on our own computer to publish our own videos to our own channels and to measure how they perform. It is not offered to the public, and no one else signs in to it. It connects only to accounts we own:

  • YouTube, through YouTube API Services: we upload our videos, titles and descriptions and read our channel's statistics and analytics (views, likes, comments, watch time, subscribers).
  • TikTok, through TikTok Login Kit and the Content Posting API: we upload our videos to our account and read our account's basic profile, follower count and video statistics.
  • Instagram and Facebook, through Meta's official APIs: we publish Reels to our professional account and Page and read their insights.

Access tokens for these accounts are stored only on our computer, in the operating system's credential manager. They are never shared, sold or sent anywhere except to the platform that issued them.

Information about viewers

We do not collect personal information about the people who watch our videos. The platforms give us aggregated statistics only (for example total views or average watch time). We do not sell, rent or share any data, and we do not use any data for advertising.

Google and YouTube

Orbit Arena Studio uses YouTube API Services. Use of YouTube is governed by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data by the Google Privacy Policy. Access granted to our tool can be revoked at any time at Google account permissions.

Revoking access

  • Google / YouTube: myaccount.google.com/permissions
  • TikTok: in the TikTok app, Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions
  • Instagram / Facebook: Settings → Apps and websites

Data deletion

To ask us to delete any data we hold that relates to you, email factoryballrace@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion". We will confirm and delete it within 30 days. Platform statistics we keep for our own reporting contain no personal information about viewers.

Retention

We keep performance statistics for as long as we need them for business reporting. Tokens are deleted as soon as an account is disconnected.

Children

Our website and tool are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We will post any change to this policy on this page and update the date above.
